Benchmark this device

Converts a generated mesh (or uses the mini you loaded) and runs three table scenes and a ramp that adds minis until the frame rate drops, about a minute. Nothing is uploaded. Paste the result into the measurements issue.

Mode: baked minis, compressed (the normal path) · baked minis, uncompressed · per-vertex look. Switching reloads the page, so load your mini afterwards.
