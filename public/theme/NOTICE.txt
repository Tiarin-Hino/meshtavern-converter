PolyWash: third-party parts and their licences
==============================================

What this page ships, each part with its licence. The page's Credits dialog lists the same
parts. The page collects nothing and sends nothing: your file is converted in your browser and
never leaves your computer, and the page stores only your own choices (the theme) on your
device.

Inter
  Fonts for text and controls: fonts/inter-latin-variable.woff2, the Latin subset of the
  variable font, weights 400 to 700.
  Copyright (c) 2016 The Inter Project Authors, https://github.com/rsms/inter
  SIL Open Font License 1.1: fonts/OFL-Inter.txt

Fraunces
  Fonts for headings: fonts/fraunces-latin-variable.woff2, the Latin subset of the variable font,
  weights 500 to 700, optical size 9 to 144.
  Copyright 2018 The Fraunces Project Authors, https://github.com/undercasetype/Fraunces
  SIL Open Font License 1.1: fonts/OFL-Fraunces.txt

Lucide 1.53.0
  Icons, bundled as inline SVG in the page from the npm package lucide-static 1.53.0.
  Copyright (c) 2026 Lucide Icons and Contributors, https://lucide.dev
  ISC License: LICENSE-Lucide.txt. The icons Lucide derived from Feather (Copyright (c)
  2013-present Cole Bemis) are also under the MIT License, whose text and list of icons are in
  the same file.

three.js 0.186.0
  Draws the minis, loads a GLB and reads the KTX2 texture, from the npm package three 0.186.0.
  Copyright (c) 2010-2026 three.js authors, https://threejs.org
  MIT License: below. Its KTX2 loader brings two parts of its own: the Basis Universal
  transcoder (/basis/, Apache License 2.0, below, with Basis Universal's notice) and zstddec
  (Copyright (c) 2019 Don McCurdy, MIT License, below), a build of Zstandard (BSD License,
  below).

meshoptimizer 1.3.0
  Reduces the minis to their detail levels and packs the compressed GLB, from the npm package
  meshoptimizer 1.3.0.
  Copyright (c) 2016-2026 Arseny Kapoulkine, https://github.com/zeux/meshoptimizer
  MIT License: below.

xatlas
  Unwraps the table level for its detail texture, compiled by this project to WebAssembly from
  upstream commit f700c779.
  Copyright (c) 2018-2020 Jonathan Young, https://github.com/jpcy/xatlas
  MIT License: below.

The Basis Universal encoder
  Encodes the detail texture as KTX2, compiled by this project to WebAssembly from upstream
  commit 1b33fd50. "Basis Universal" is a trademark of Binomial LLC.
  Copyright (c) 2016-2026 Binomial LLC, https://github.com/BinomialLLC/basis_universal
  Apache License 2.0: below, with Basis Universal's notice. The build includes Zstandard
  (Copyright (c) 2016-present, Facebook, Inc., BSD License, below) and the QOI and DDS
  readers Basis Universal bundles (MIT License, below).

System Reference Document 5.1
  The creature size names (Tiny, Small, Medium, Large, Huge, Gargantuan).
  This work includes material taken from the System Reference Document 5.1 ("SRD 5.1") by
  Wizards of the Coast LLC and available at
  https://dnd.wizards.com/resources/systems-reference-document. The SRD 5.1 is licensed under
  the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License available at
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode.

Not a third-party part, but credited here:

The favicon
  The letter P of PolyWash, set in Fraunces (above), until the page has its own mark.

The page itself is under the MIT License: https://github.com/Tiarin-Hino/meshtavern-converter
