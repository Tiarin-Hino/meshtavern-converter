## What we did

Phase 1 ended on 2026-10-02: every story of the epic (#40) is merged, and the PM signed off the exit criteria. The spec now says, criterion by criterion, how each one ended, and lists the phase's journal entries in reading order. Nothing in the converter's behaviour changed with this entry.

## Why

A status look on 2026-10-02 found every linked issue closed but the epic still open, with four points the documents called open: the largest corpus file on the reference laptop, the PM's look at the comparison sheets, the PM's visual call on the page (#41) and the PM's choice of the look presets' values (#45). The PM answered all four the same day.

## How

- **The largest file on the laptop left the phase.** The spec proposed 3 minutes or less for the 281 MB corpus file on the Intel Iris Xe laptop; it was only ever run on the development PC (114 s). The PM moved the run out of the exit criteria and made it a release blocker, #103, to be done later with the laptop at hand.
- **The comparison sheets were looked at.** The PM marked every corpus group in the marking session of #93 (`npm run feedback -- --mark`), which shows each mini.
- **A merged PR is the PM's yes.** The page (PR #80) and the presets (PR #102) were listed as "Open: PM" in the spec although the PM had merged both. The PM's rule from now on: only the PM merges, so a merged PR means its proposals were accepted, or changed before the merge. `CLAUDE.md` carries the rule, and the two "Open" lines became "accepted by merging".
- **One exit criterion said something that was no longer true.** It asked for "an upright … mini without manual help". On PR #79 (2026-09-25) the PM had decided that detection is a help and the person's placement is final, after the stance score stood 1 of 17 baseless corpus minis upright. The criterion now describes what was built: detected, shown before anything converts, and the answer is final.

## Problems and how we solved them

- **Problem.** The documents kept points "open" that the PM considered closed. **Cause:** a status line was written when the PR was opened and nobody changed it at the merge. **Fix:** the merge rule above, so a later reader does not have to ask.
